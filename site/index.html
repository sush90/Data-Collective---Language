    Try your voice

    Read a sentence aloud in any language for about 5 seconds. We compute your prosodic fingerprint and place it on the map among the languages it sounds most like.

    Your audio never leaves this device. Everything is computed in your browser and nothing is uploaded or stored. The tool measures only rhythm and melody. It does not identify who you are, and it does not guess age, gender or anything else about you.
